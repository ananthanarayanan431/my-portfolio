building one app every week, hoping the next one is going to work out!

It's a gambler mentality, multiple similar failure point and it's like you will be doing the same series of mistake again and again. not learning from it (here i'm talking about tech though)

refusing to update to you model! (not ai!)

so, what's next? so build a product that people love!

If a product is built for everybody, it's like a love letter addressed "To Whom It May Concern." So, when you're building something, make sure you're building it for one person who is going to absolutely love it!

make a product that you care about, not just something you're excited about! 😅
