3,000+ developers in one convention hall on a Saturday.

That was DevFest Hyderabad 2026, and it easily became my favourite day of the month.

## Workshops, after the queue

The day started the way every big event starts: a long wait in the registration queue. 😅

Once I got in, I spent most of the day in tech workshops covering **Google ADK**, **Neo4j** and **GraphRAG**.

<figure>
  <Image src={aiEconomyTalk} alt="A speaker presenting a slide about AI's role in Telangana's economy" />
  <figcaption>One of the sessions from the main hall.</figcaption>
</figure>

In between, I spoke with a lot of developers about what each of us is building, and met aspiring software engineers from different colleges.

## The one question I kept asking

I asked almost every developer I met the same question:

> *"What do you think the future of software engineering looks like?"*

Most of them were optimistic. Their answers were roughly the same:

**The job isn't going away, but the way we work has already changed.**

## Conversations worth the trip

We got the chance to discuss our idea with **Srini Chilukuri**, founder of TensorGo, and I'm really thankful for the insights and guidance he gave us.

We also got to speak with **Karthik Padmanabhan**, and had an insightful conversation with **Shrreya Behll** about self-hosting different LLMs.

## The best part

Honestly, the best part of the day was spending it with **Deepak Vaduganambi** and **Virender Garg**.

We don't get many full days together outside work. 😇 So a Saturday of meeting people, arguing about ideas and eating conference lunch together was a good one.
